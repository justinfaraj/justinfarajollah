Hi, I'm Justin

I'm a student at Indiana University's Kelley School of Business, and I build websites for small businesses.

It started two years ago as a one-time thing. I loved it, people started finding me, and I kept doing it. I've built a lot of websites since then, and helping small business owners get a site they're proud of is still my favorite part.

If you need a site, or the one you have isn't pulling its weight, book a chat or send me a text and we'll talk through it.

What I do

Everything a small business needs to get online, handled by one person.

Design

A clean, professional look that fits your business and makes a good first impression, on phones and computers.

Build

A fast, simple site with the pages and information your customers are actually looking for.

Writing help

Not sure what to say? I'll help you shape the words so your site sounds like you and is easy to read.

Launch and changes

I get it live on your own address, and you can reach me if you need something updated later.

How it works

  1. 1

    We talk

    Book a chat or send a text. Tell me about your business and what you want the site to do.

  2. 2

    I design it

    I put together a design for you to react to, and we adjust it until it feels right.

  3. 3

    It goes live

    I build and launch the site, and you have a website you're proud to share.
